Refyne

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version. Refyne stores what you log so you can track your progress. Your data is kept in Sydney, other users can't see it, and we don't run ads or sell your data. You can download your data or delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings.

Who we are

Refyne ("we", "us") is a fitness and nutrition tracking app available at refynefit.net and as an iPhone app. It is run by Alex Sowry, a sole trader based in Queensland, Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Questions or requests: hello@refynefit.net.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored by our database provider in hashed form; we never see them.
  • Your setup: details you enter to work out your targets, such as age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal, plus your settings and theme.
  • What you log: food, drinks, water, supplements, steps, workouts, cardio, weigh-ins, body measurements, notes and progress photos.
  • Things you share on purpose: products or supplements you add from a nutrition label, reports of wrong product information, and feedback you send us.
  • Technical information: the app version, and, if you turn on rest-timer notifications in the web app, a notification address for your device. Our hosting providers may also keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times.

Weights, body measurements, food logs and progress photos are health information, which the Privacy Act treats as sensitive. We only collect it with your consent, which you give when you create an account, and only use it to run the app for you.

We don't collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To show your logs, totals, targets, charts and progress across your devices.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password resets.
  • To send rest-timer notifications, if you turn them on.
  • To answer feedback and fix problems.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who can see it

Your logs, measurements and photos are private to your account. The database uses row-level security, so other users can't read them, and progress photos are kept in private storage.

Products and supplements you add from a nutrition label are shared with all Refyne users so everyone can log them. These show the product's name, brand and nutrition values only, never your name or email.

On your device

Refyne saves some data on your phone so it opens quickly and works offline, such as an unsaved workout or your recent settings. Barcode scanning happens on your device: camera images are never uploaded. Progress photos are only uploaded when you choose to add one, and they're resized first.

Keeping it and deleting it

  • We keep your data for as long as you have an account.
  • Delete your account any time from Settings. This permanently deletes your logs, measurements, settings and progress photos. Copies in our providers' backups are removed as those backups expire.
  • Products you added to the shared list stay available to other users, but they're no longer linked to you.

Your rights

  • Access and download: see everything in the app, or download a full copy from Settings.
  • Correct: edit or delete any entry in the app, or ask us to fix anything you can't change yourself.
  • Ask questions or complain: email hello@refynefit.net and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to your data is limited to your account, and our admin accounts use two-factor sign-in. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the OAIC as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

Age

Refyne is for people aged 16 and over. If you believe someone younger has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Not medical advice

Refyne is a general fitness and wellness tracker. Its numbers are estimates and it doesn't diagnose, treat or manage any medical condition. See the Terms of Use.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll let you know in the app before it takes effect.
